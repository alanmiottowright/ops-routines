Rocket.Chat triage · redesign

RC Briefing: plan and mockup

The 4 pm triage stops being a silent cleanup and becomes a briefing. It posts one Rocket.Chat thread to you each weekday, modelled on the Ops Team sprint thread, and publishes the same briefing as a page in Claude. It sorts everything into four tiers, so things you should read still reach you even when nobody asked you anything.

Waiting for your approval

What changes

  • One briefing thread per run. The parent message is a bold headline with the counts. The thread replies hold one section per tier, with a direct link to every message that matters.
  • Four tiers instead of two. Today a room is either held or marked read, and anything marked read disappears. The new middle tiers, "Worth reading" and "Skim", keep useful FYI in front of you.
  • Room profiles you can edit. A new file, context/rc-room-profiles.md, gives each busy room a default tier and the events that should lift it. For example, sales-alerts stays quiet unless a deal is $350K or more.
  • A briefing page in Claude at a fixed private link. It shows the same content with done-checkboxes and the last 14 briefings.
  • One new write is allowed. The routine may post to your briefing DM and nowhere else. Every other room stays read-only, apart from marking rooms read.

Backfill test, 9–18 Nov

  1. One run of the new triage over every room with traffic since Mon 9 Nov, 00:00 ET. That's 99 rooms: 63 private groups, 18 channels and 18 DMs. It reads the full message history for the period, not just what's unread.
  2. It changes nothing in Rocket.Chat except posting the briefing. It doesn't mark rooms read or unread, so last week's state stays as you left it. The regular 4 pm run carries on as normal afterwards.
  3. It adds a "Possibly missed" section. This lists every @alan.wright ask or DM in the window that got no reply from you and nobody else picked up, including rooms the old triage had already marked read. This is the "nothing key was missed" check.
  4. It posts one briefing, [RC Briefing] Backfill 9–18 Nov, to the DM and as its own tab on the page. You review it, and we adjust the room profiles before the daily runs rely on them.

It runs as a one-off cloud run on Sonnet. 99 rooms is a long read, so expect it to take 20–40 minutes.

Decisions for you

1. Who sends the briefing

Recommended: a DM to yourself, posted with your existing cloud-triage token. It works today, but Rocket.Chat doesn't notify you about your own messages, so the routine also sends a phone push through Claude with the page link. Alternative: a bot account such as ops.briefing that DMs you and shows as unread. That needs a workspace admin to create the bot and its token. You can switch to the bot later without redesigning anything.

2. Cap on "Worth reading": set to 20

At most 20 per run, ranked by importance, with any overflow listed under Skim. #important posts don't count toward it. We'll refine the number over time.

3. Run time: keep 4 pm ET

It stays at 4 pm ET on weekdays (21:00 UTC) while we see how it goes. Adding a morning run later just means a second schedule time. The routine already handles whatever is unread since the last run, so nothing else changes.
